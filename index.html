About me

I'm Abhisek Acharya, a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) student at Tribhuvan University, Nepal, and a primary-level computer teacher.

My work covers teaching computer fundamentals, practical IT support and software development. I enjoy the parts people don't see: databases, backend systems and computer networks.

I want to keep improving as a developer, build digital tools people can use, and keep sharing what I learn with my students. I also make teaching materials in Nepali.
